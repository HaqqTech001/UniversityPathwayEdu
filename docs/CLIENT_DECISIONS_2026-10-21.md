# UPEC Confirmed Institutional Requirements — 21 October 2026

Approved in direct client requirements discussion; not inferred from the sample filled-in form.

## Admissions
- Student account can submit **separate applications for IJMB, JAMB, WAEC and other offered programmes** after completing all applicable requirements. One account must not be limited to one programme.
- All programmes share a common comprehensive application data schema and wizard, with programme-specific subject and admission options where necessary.
- **Student registration numbers are generated only following admissions approval**, using independent year/programme sequences. Examples: `26/UPIJMB/001`, `26/UPJAMB/001`, `26/UPWAEC/001`. Application references remain separate.
- Registration-number schema must be reviewed with management for year rollover, programme naming, number length and existing paper records.

## Approved application fee schedules
- IJMB: **NGN 25,000**
- JAMB: **NGN 15,000**
- WAEC: **NGN 15,000**
- Authorized administrators can add new effective-dated rates without editing historical charges.
- These are **application fees**, not recurring monthly lesson fees. Determine effective date and exact programme-code mapping before publishing rates in production.

## Student policies
- The current official policy is the **14-rule Students' Code of Conduct poster**.
- Superseded rules in the old A'Level paper form should not be activated as current policies.
- Versioned acknowledgement should precede final application submission.

## Accommodation and services
- Include a hostel/accommodation interest and request workflow in the common application.
- Availability, allocation, pricing, eligibility, photographs and hostel rules are to be supplied and approved by management. Do not charge students or promise rooms without confirmation.
- Capture student biodata, guardian, emergency contact, sponsor, academic history, chosen course/institutions, declaration and document requirements, following the client-supplied paper form, with privacy safeguards.

## Domain
- **pathwayedu.com** selected as the desired public domain. Domain ownership/availability and registration are still to be verified. No claim has been made that it has been registered.

## Operational readiness
- Do not equate successful code commits or migrations with acceptance-tested production functionality.
- Validate application flows, permissions, year/programme sequences, fee schedules and notification delivery end-to-end before launch.
